Chapter 1

Signing in

Open the tracker's web address in any browser. You'll see the sign-in page. There are two ways in.

With a Google account

  1. Click Continue with Google.
  2. Pick your Google account in the window that opens. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the site and try again.

With email and password

  1. Type your email and password, then click Sign in (or press Enter).
  2. No account yet? Type your email and a password of at least six characters and click Create account.
  3. Forgot it? Type your email and click Forgot password. A reset link arrives by email.

If an admin set up your login

When an admin creates your account with a temporary password, the first sign-in takes you to a Choose your own password screen. Type a new password twice and click Save password. From then on, use the new one.

What you can see depends on your role. New accounts start as General: Overview, Reports, Documents and Calendar, view only, no dollar amounts. An admin can give you more (see Admin). If a section you expect is missing from the sidebar, ask an admin.

Signing out: click Sign out at the bottom of the sidebar. On a shared computer, always sign out.

Chapter 2

The basics: screen, records, editing

Every section works the same way. Learn it once here and it applies to Donors, Donations, Events, Phases, Workers and Tasks.

Parts of the screen

  • Sidebar — every section you have access to, with a record count. Click one to open it.
  • Search box — type any part of a name, organization, ID or note. The list narrows as you type.
  • + New button — starts a new record in the current section.
  • Totals strip — counts and dollar totals for what's in the list.
  • Filters and Sort — narrow the list (status, payment type, owner, task, type) and change its order. Status and Payment type let you tick more than one box.
  • Connection light — bottom of the sidebar. Live · Firestore means changes are saving and reaching everyone else within a second. Offline · cached means you can keep working; changes send when the connection returns.

Opening a record

Click any line in a list. The record opens in a window over the list, showing its totals and every field. Close it with Close or the red × in the corner.

Adding a record

  1. Click + New at the top right of the section.
  2. Fill in the fields. Fields marked required must have a value before it will save.
  3. Click Save. Click Cancel to throw the entry away.

Editing a record

  1. Open the record and click Edit entry.
  2. Change what you need and click Save.

All changes happen inside the Edit entry window, including payment schedules, Log contact, Log reminder and Delete. If you don't see Edit entry, your role is view only for that section.

Field types you'll meet

  • Dollar fields format as you type: enter 1500000 and it shows $1,500,000.
  • Dropdowns with "Add a new option" — pick from the list, or type a new value and click Add. It's offered on every later entry.
  • Link fields (Donor, Linked to, Assigned to, Funded by) — type part of a name and pick the record from the list. This is what connects sections together.
  • Tags — type a tag and click Add. Tags are shared across donors, donations and tasks.

Deleting

Open the record, click Edit entry, then Delete. You'll be asked to confirm with Delete permanently or back out with Keep it. Deleting can't be undone.

Chapter 3

Overview

The Overview is the campaign dashboard. It is read only; every figure is calculated from the other sections.

TileWhat it counts
Received to dateMoney actually in hand, including installment payments already received.
PledgedThe remaining balance on pledges — what's committed in writing but not yet received. Payments already in are not counted twice.
PromisedVerbal commitments.
PotentialOpen asks plus high-priority donors. "Throwing darts" donations count here.
Funding gapFull cost less received, pledged and promised.
Full costThe estimated cost of every task across all phases.

Campaign against full cost is one bar the length of the full cost, filled left to right with Received (dark green), Pledged (green), Promised (gold), Potential (pink) and the Funding gap (red).

NKU Grant Master Tracker opens the top fifty open asks, ranked by probability or dollars, with deadline, contact, portal link, match and owner. It exports to PDF or Excel.

Needs attention lists anything due in the next two weeks or up to two weeks overdue. Upcoming events and Recent activity show what's next and what changed. Click any line to open it.

Chapter 4 · Section 01

Donors

One record per person, family or organization that gives or might give. Donations are entered separately (chapter 5) and roll up onto the donor automatically.

Reading the list

Each line shows the donor ID and type, the name, the account owner, and four columns: Potential, Promised, Pledged and Received, with the donor's current status underneath. Pledged shows the full commitment. The line color tells you where the donor stands at a glance:

Green — has given something (Received or In Funding Progress)
Lighter green — pledged, nothing received yet
Light yellow — promised only
White — no commitment yet

Use Top 10, Status, Payment type and account owner to filter; the Sort buttons order by donations or potential; the Minimum donation total slider hides smaller donors.

Adding a donor

  1. Click + New donor.
  2. Choose the Type (Individual, Organization, Family) and enter the Name.
  3. Add contact details and Preferred contact.
  4. Set Priority and Current status, and pick an Account owner — the team member who owns the relationship. Donations with nobody assigned inherit this person.
  5. Optionally connect the donor: Task group / location, Linked to (family members, a business, a matching source) and Phase / task connection.
  6. Click Save.

Contacts, reminders and recognition

  • Log contact (inside Edit entry) records a call, meeting or email with a date and note.
  • Log reminder sets a follow-up date and owner. It appears on the Calendar in gold.
  • Donor tiers are earned from total received. When a donor reaches a new tier, a recognition certificate is offered on their record until it's printed.
Chapter 5 · Section 02

Donations

Every gift, pledge, grant application and ask is a donation record, always tied to one donor.

Statuses

StatusUse it when
ReceivedThe money is in. Date received fills in automatically.
PledgedA firm, usually written, commitment not yet paid.
PromisedA verbal commitment.
PotentialAn open ask or application.
Throwing dartsA long shot. Counts as Potential in the totals.

Adding a donation

  1. Click + New donation.
  2. Optionally choose a Title/Event (a grant or event name). Reusing a title groups several donors together.
  3. In Donor, type part of the name, organization or ID and pick the donor.
  4. Enter the Amount, Date entered (the day you create the entry) and, if there is one, the Deadline. For a pledge or promise, the Deadline is the follow-up date: it appears on the Calendar and in Overview → Needs attention as "Pledge follow up" or "Promise follow up".
  5. Choose the Status, Probability, NKU team assigned and Payment type.
  6. For grants, fill in contact, application link, match required and eligible NKU component.
  7. Click Save.

Installment donations

  1. Set Donation type to Installment.
  2. Choose the Installment frequency (Monthly or Annual), Number of payments and First payment date.
  3. Save. The amount is split into a payment schedule.
  4. As each payment arrives, open the donation, click Edit entry and tick that payment off in the Payment schedule.

The status follows the schedule on its own: Pledged while nothing is received, In Funding Progress once some payments are in, and Received when the last one is ticked.

Certificates

Open a donation and click Donor certificate to preview a certificate of appreciation, then Print certificate.

Chapter 6 · Section 03

Events

Galas, dinners, raffles and other gatherings, with their attendees, tasks, documents and the gifts taken there.

Creating an event

  1. Click + New event.
  2. Enter the Event name — it also becomes the donation title and tag for every gift taken at the event.
  3. Set Date & time, address, Hosting company (from the Workers roster) and a Linked site if there's a registration page.
  4. Save, then open Edit entry to add attendees (pick existing donors or type new names), Tasks to do with an assignee, and Documents such as fliers and guides.

At and after the event

  • + Add donation records a gift taken at the event, with its status (Received, Pledged or Promised). It appears in Donations and on the donor automatically.
  • Post event donations is a check, not a transfer. It confirms every event gift appears once in Donations and Donors, removes any duplicate, and stamps the event as verified. Running it twice changes nothing.
  • Print attendee list, Print task list and Print certificates (one per donor with Received or Pledged gifts) print straight from the event.
Chapter 7 · Section 04

Phases of work

The big stages of the project. Each phase has a budget, dates, a status and a lead. Tasks belong to phases, so a phase's progress and funding come from its tasks.

  1. Click + New phase.
  2. Enter the Phase name and Budget, then Start and Target finish.
  3. Set Status (Not started, In progress, Blocked, Complete) and pick a Phase lead from the Workers roster.
  4. Save. Phase start and finish dates appear on the Calendar as milestones.
Chapter 8 · Section 05

Workers & contractors

Everyone who does the work: contractors, employees, volunteers and donated labor.

  1. Click + New worker.
  2. Enter the name, pick or add a Company, and choose the Status (Contacted through Contracted) and Trade / role.
  3. Add rate, contact details, license and Insurance through date.
  4. If this person also gives, point Also a donor at their donor record.
  5. Save.

Companies added here are offered as Hosting company on events.

Chapter 9 · Section 06

Work to do / tasks

The individual pieces of work. Task costs add up to the project's full cost, so this section drives the Funding gap.

  1. Click + New task.
  2. Enter the Task name and choose its Type.
  3. Pick the Phase and a Task group / location (or add a new group right there).
  4. Set Status, Start date, Due date and/or Duration.
  5. Choose who it's Assigned to (workers) and the NKU team assigned owner.
  6. Enter the Estimated cost, and in Funded by pick the donors whose giving is earmarked for it.
  7. Save. In Edit entry you can also add Subtasks with their own due dates.

Chapter 11 · Section 10

Reading the calendar

The Calendar gathers every dated item from the other sections. You don't enter anything here; it fills itself.

What the colors mean

Subtask due
Event

Donor follow-up: reminders, and pledge or promise deadlines

Controls

  • ← → move back or forward a month (or a week). Today jumps back to now.
  • Month / Week switches the view. Week shows more of each label.
  • Anyone assigned filters to one person — choose your own name to see only your items.
  • Event tasks: off/on adds the tasks attached to events.
  • When a day has more items than fit, it shows +2 (or similar). Click an item to see everything on that day and open it.
Chapter 12 · Section 08

Reading reports

Reports are live charts built from your records. Click any report to expand it; from the expanded view you can print or export it.

Ask for a report

  1. Type a question in plain words, such as "Donations by status" or "Total donations by month in 2026 as a bar graph". Or click one of the example buttons.
  2. Click Run. Say "bar graph" for a chart, and "by month" or "by status" to group.
  3. Click Expand, print or export to open it full size. Clear resets it.

If nothing comes back, try fewer words, or spell a name exactly as it appears on the record.

The standing reports

ReportHow to read it
Donations receivedA running total over time. The line only goes up; steeper means more money arrived in that stretch.
Daily givingOne bar per day for the month: how much was received that day. Tall bars are big days.
Phase swim lanesEach phase as a bar across the quarters it spans. Overlapping bars run at the same time.
Funding gap by phaseEach phase's budget as a full-width bar, with the donations allocated to it filled in from the left. The unfilled part is that phase's gap.
Pledged vs. receivedHow much of what's been pledged has actually come in.
Top donorsThe largest donors by total giving.
Open donations by ownerOpen asks grouped by the team member responsible. Useful for follow-up meetings.
Phase completionHow far each phase has progressed, from its tasks' status.

Reports print landscape. If amounts show as dashes, your role doesn't include View $$.

Chapter 13 · Section 09

Documents

A shared library of files: how-to guides, timelines, fliers, ideas. Files uploaded to an event also appear here.

  • + Add Document — choose a type, give it a title and description, and upload the file.
  • Search, the type filter and Newest first help you find a file.
  • Download saves a copy. Edit changes the title, type or file.
  • For event files, Edit in Original Location opens the event they belong to.
Chapter 14

Using it on a phone

The tracker works in a phone's browser with the same login. The layout changes to fit.

  • The sidebar becomes a menu drawer. Tap the menu button at the top left to open it and pick a section.
  • A bottom bar gives one-tap access to the most used sections.
  • Tap a line to open the record full screen; use the back arrow at the top left to return to the list.
  • Add the site to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android) to open it like an app.
  • No signal at an event? Keep entering. Changes are saved on the phone and send when you're back online. Check the connection light in the menu before you close the browser.
  • Printing attendee lists, certificates and reports is easier from a computer.
Chapter 15 · Section 07

Admin

For people with the Admin role. Admin controls who can sign in, what they can see and change, and the lists and fields every other section uses. It is arranged as tabs across the top.

Users & invites

To add a user:

  1. Click + Add user.
  2. Enter their Name and Email, and tick their roles (General is preselected).
  3. Optionally type a temporary password (six characters or more). Leave it blank if they'll sign in with Google or create their own password.
  4. Click Add user, then give them the email and temporary password.

A user with a temporary password shows a Temp password tag until they sign in and choose their own; they can't get past that screen until they do.

  • Edit — change name, roles or add extra operations for that one person.
  • Deactivate — blocks sign-in but keeps the account and history. Reactivate reverses it.
  • × — removes the account entirely.
  • You can't remove your own admin access; another admin has to.

Team members without a login (under Tags & lists) are board members or volunteers who own donations or tasks but don't use the tracker. They appear in owner and assignee lists.

Roles & permissions

A role is a named bundle of permissions. Each section can be Off, View only or Read / write, and View $$ decides whether dollar amounts reach that person's device at all. A user gets the highest level any of their roles (or extra operations) gives.

RoleGives
AdminEverything, including Admin and View $$. Locked.
GeneralView only: Overview, Reports, Documents, Calendar. Amounts hidden. Every new account starts here.
View OnlyView only on every section except Admin. Amounts hidden.
Your ownType a name (e.g. Event Crew) and click + Add role, then set each section.

Data fields

Controls the fields on every entry form. Pick a section (Donors, Donations, Events…), then:

  • Tick Required to make a field mandatory.
  • Choices edits the options of a dropdown field.
  • ↑ moves a field up the form; × removes it.
  • Add a field on the right: give it a label and type, then Add field. It appears on that section's form straight away.

Task groups

A task group is a place or sub-project — a field, a wing, the utility corridor. Each task belongs to one group, and a group can span several phases.

  • Type a name and click Add group. Click a name to rename it.
  • Phases sets which phases the group covers.
  • Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder. × deletes.

Tags & lists

The shared lists behind dropdowns: Tags, Team members without a login, Donation titles and Companies. Add a value, drag to reorder, or remove one with ×. Each shows how many records use it; removing a value in use doesn't delete those records.

Donor tiers

Recognition levels by total received. Edit a tier's name or minimum amount in place; tiers sort by amount automatically. A donor earns the highest tier their received total reaches, shown as a badge, and a certificate is offered on their record until it's printed.

Bulk upload

  1. Pick the section to load (Donors, Donations, Events, Phases, Workers, Tasks).
  2. Click Download template. It's a CSV with the right column headers; starred columns are required, and a guide row shows the expected format.
  3. Fill it in with Excel or Google Sheets and save as CSV.
  4. Click Upload CSV. You'll see a preview and a count before anything is written.

Names in link columns are matched to records already in the tracker, so load in this order: donors and phases, then workers and task groups, then tasks, then donations.

Text size: Admin → General sets how large the desktop interface appears (100–150%). It's remembered per browser and doesn't affect phones or other users.
