Try in the mockup
Admin → Data fields → add a field to Donors, then open Donors → New: the form picks it up. Every list row opens a detail pane; detail panes cross-link to related records.
Data model assumed
Transactions carry donorId. Tasks carry phaseId, a workerIds list and dated cost rows. Donors and transactions each carry a taskIds list, so one gift can be split across tasks. A worker record can point at a donor record.
Open questions
Should allocation of a gift across tasks be by amount or by percentage? Do workers need timesheets, or only assignment? Who may see donor amounts — role-gated, or all signed-in users?
